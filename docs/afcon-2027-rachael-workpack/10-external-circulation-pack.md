## 4. Procurement and timing intelligence

The LOC has publicly stated that its supplier pre-qualification exercise is part of procurement planning for AFCON PAMOJA 2027. The July 2026 public notice said future tenders were expected in September–October 2026 and listed event management, consultancy services, documentary production, fan zones/park management, media/communications and production among relevant categories.

1. a specialist creative consultancy / creative-direction proposition;

3. a specialist creative subcontractor to an already-qualified agency or supplier.

Rachael's individual professional status and any Uganda-based contracting entity should be clarified before any procurement submission.
