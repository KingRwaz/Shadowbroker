# Rachael Nanyonjo × AFCON PAMOJA 2027 — Visual Direction

## Concept
**UGANDA: THE CULTURE BEHIND THE GAME**

The visual identity should communicate three ideas simultaneously: **movement, place and international creative authority**. It should feel editorial and premium rather than like a generic football poster.

## Design language
- **Base:** deep midnight/navy field for an executive, cinematic presentation environment.
- **Accent:** warm gold for energy, hospitality, sunlight and premium experience.
- **Signal:** restrained red for movement, urgency and African performance energy.
- **Typography:** large contemporary sans-serif headlines; generous tracking for labels; short copy blocks.
- **Imagery:** Rachael’s real portfolio photography should replace abstract figure imagery whenever licensed/available.
- **Graphic motif:** an orbit/arc represents football, audience movement and cultural exchange; a rhythmic landscape line suggests Uganda as destination.
- **Composition:** asymmetrical editorial layouts with generous negative space and one strong focal image.

## Recommended presentation system
1. Cover — hero portrait/action image + title.
2. Credentials — three proof points + selected credits.
3. Opportunity — AFCON ecosystem map.
4. Proposition — six service pillars.
5. Flagship concept — Uganda: The Culture Behind the Game.
6. Experience modules — welcome, fan culture, hospitality, diaspora, youth, destination.
7. Pilot — 30/60/90-day activation.
8. Partnership — what Rachael needs from the stakeholder.
9. Contact — minimal, premium closing page.

## Asset rules
- Never imply official CAF/AFCON endorsement unless formally authorised.
- Do not recreate or modify official AFCON/PAMOJA logos as part of Rachael’s independent identity.
- Use licensed photography and credit photographers/productions appropriately.
- Keep the Rachael mark/name independent from tournament marks.
- Every stakeholder-specific version should be adapted to its audience rather than sent as a generic deck.

## Core message
> Rachael Nanyonjo brings international directing, choreography and movement expertise to the design of culturally distinctive visitor experiences for Uganda’s AFCON 2027 moment.
