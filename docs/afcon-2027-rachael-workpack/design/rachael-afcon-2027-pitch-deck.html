THE PROPOSITION

Culture is not the interval.
It is the experience.

AFCON can be a platform through which visitors encounter Uganda through movement, music, theatre, fashion, food, design, storytelling and hospitality.

ATTRACT

Create cultural moments that give visitors a reason to arrive early, stay longer and explore beyond the stadium.

CONNECT

Build shared experiences between football audiences, Ugandan communities and international visitors.

REMEMBER

Design experiences that become part of what people remember—and share—about Uganda.

Core proposition: culture-led visitor experience, designed with professional artistic direction.
